# Phase 1 Journeys

Viewport 1440×900 unless noted. Start every journey after "Reset demo".

- **JRN-1** First glance. Steps: open dashboard. Expected: header shows "Demo West Freight", "4 trucks", "Mon 26 Oct 2026 · 10:00 PT", "Demo data", "Talk to FreightOS". Cards show $3,700 / $1,054 ("$2,646 direct costs · Before overhead") / 29.9% ("440 of 1,470 planned miles"). Map shows 4 truck markers labelled 101–104 inside the view. Attention strip shows "Delivery at risk · Truck 102" and "Return load ready · Truck 101". AI summary line is visible.
- **JRN-2** Map legibility. Steps: view map at 1280, 1440 and 1920 widths. Expected: SF, Oakland, Sacramento, Stockton, Fresno, San Jose and LA labels are readable, truck labels don't overlap, attribution is visible, the legend shows confirmed / proposed / empty repositioning, and the command bar covers no map controls.
- **JRN-3** Truck select/deselect. Steps: hover Truck 103, click it, then press Esc. Expected: hover tooltip shows "Chloe Chen" with an on-track status. Click opens a panel with "53' dry van", L-301 Sacramento→Oakland, ETA 11:30, window 11:00–13:00, and the other routes dim. Esc closes the panel and restores all routes.
- **JRN-4** Truck 101 panel. Steps: click Truck 101. Expected: panel shows truck release Mon 12:00 and driver available Tue 07:00 as separate fields, plus L-103 Wed 08:00–09:00 SF pickup marked "Protected commitment". Current position and future stops use different symbols.
- **JRN-5** Metric breakdown. Steps: click "Empty driving". Expected: breakdown lists L-101 15, L-103 10, L-201 10, L-301 5 and the 400 mi empty return = 440 of 1,470. Clicking "After trip costs (est.)" shows $3,700 − $2,646 = $1,054 at $1.80/mi.
- **JRN-6** Current plan explainer. Steps: open "Current plan" info. Expected: text mentions active trips, confirmed pickups in the next 48 h, connecting repositioning, and that revenue is not cash collected.
- **JRN-7** Ask AI. Steps: click chip "What needs me?". Expected: conversation panel opens with 2 items linked to their decisions. Clicking the Truck 101 link opens the return-load decision.
- **JRN-8** Unknown command. Steps: type "sing a song" and submit. Expected: fallback reply lists the supported example commands.
- **JRN-9** Voice. Steps: click the header mic. Expected: a waveform shows "Listening…", then the transcribed command appears, then the scripted answer. Clicking the mic again mid-listen cancels it with no message sent.
- **JRN-10** Decision panel content. Steps: click "Return load ready · Truck 101". Expected: Truck 101 is selected and the panel leads with LA→Oakland $1,100, "+$1,046" (labelled planned contribution, not extra revenue), and "In SF Tue 18:00". Economics show 430 mi, $774, $326 vs -$720. Alternatives show LA–Phoenix $1,600 as unsuitable (conflicts with Wed L-103). Terms show 30,000 lb packaged goods and "Written terms match offer" linked to the rate confirmation. The proposed route is dashed on the map.
- **JRN-11** Preview before booking. Steps: open the decision and click "Approve and request confirmation". Expected: status is "Awaiting broker". Cards still show $3,700 / $1,054 / 29.9%, and a labelled "Preview" shows $4,800 / $2,100 / 4.7%.
- **JRN-12** Broker call. Steps: after approving, watch the call panel. Expected: contact "Demo Pacific Broker", an objective, progressive transcript lines, extracted facts (rate $1,100, PU/DEL windows), and an outcome "Confirmed in writing". Pause stops the transcript and Resume continues it.
- **JRN-13** Booking effect. Steps: let the call finish. Expected: status is "Booked". Cards show $4,800 / $2,100 ("$2,700 direct costs") / 4.7% ("70 of 1,500 planned miles"). The Truck 101 route becomes LA→Oakland→SF and the empty return is gone. "Return load ready" leaves the attention strip, and the activity feed and notifications show the booking.
- **JRN-14** Driver acknowledgment. Steps: continue after booking. Expected: "Driver instructions sent" appears, then "Driver acknowledged" (Ava Reed), shown separately from "Booked". The stepper reaches its final step.
- **JRN-15** Idempotency. Steps: triple-click approve quickly, and reload mid-call. Expected: exactly one approval, one call, one booking, one driver message and one set of activity entries. Metrics are not double-counted.
- **JRN-16** Reject needs reason. Steps: in a fresh demo, click Reject. Expected: the confirm button is disabled until a reason is typed. After rejecting, status is "Rejected" with the reason in the activity feed and the metrics unchanged.
- **JRN-17** Take over. Steps: in a fresh demo, click "Take over" in the decision panel. Expected: "You're handling this" shows and the AI starts no call. In the call panel, Take over marks the call "Dispatcher took over".
- **JRN-18** Request changes. Steps: click Request changes, type a note and submit. Expected: status is "Changes requested" with an activity entry.
- **JRN-19** Truck 102 risk. Steps: click "Delivery at risk · Truck 102". Expected: Truck 102 is selected and the panel shows release 12:30, ETA 15:30 vs window 14:00–15:00, and "Revised appointment requested", all in amber/red.
- **JRN-20** Notifications. Steps: open the bell. Expected: the "Needs you" group and the "Updates" group are separate, and clicking an item opens its truck or decision.
- **JRN-21** Reset. Steps: complete the booking, click Reset demo and confirm. Expected: everything returns to the JRN-1 state. Cancelling the confirmation changes nothing.
- **JRN-22** Persistence. Steps: book, then reload the page. Expected: booked state and metrics persist.
- **JRN-23** Fleet placeholder (interpreted). Steps: click Fleet in the nav. Expected: a read-only list of 4 trucks with drivers and statuses. Dashboard nav returns to the map.
- **JRN-24** Schedule placeholder (interpreted). Steps: select Truck 101 and switch to Schedule. Expected: a "Coming in next phase" placeholder with Truck 101 still selected. Switching back to Map keeps the selection.
- **JRN-25** Keyboard. Steps: Tab through the header, cards, attention strip, command bar and decision actions. Expected: each has a visible focus ring and Enter activates it.
